Each week identifies a **60-minute Monday lecture selection** with slide
numbers and timings. Week 1 uses its complete introductory deck and has no
tutorial or separate deliverable.

In weeks 2–12, the lecture is followed by a **90-minute Thursday tutorial**
and **about 210 minutes of independent work**, including preparation,
remaining worked examples and assessment work. The weekly plans name those
tasks. The larger decks and notes remain the complete teaching resources;
their 180-minute reference routes add no extra scheduled session.
The [toolkit](/toolkit/) supplies shared data and executable examples.
