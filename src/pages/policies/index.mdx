## The lab boundary

All datasets and case profiles supplied here are invented. No assignment requires
an app account, a real date, personal disclosure, scraping, automated swiping or
contacting a stranger. Do not submit real profiles, private messages, location
histories or identifiable data. Keep optional pair review about fictional
artefacts; solo implementation and review meet the same criteria.

Pink and blue distinguish course accents, not genders. Model states are not a
required account of anyone's identity or relationships. Do not rank classmates
or treat the class as a dating pool.

## The strict “No Feelings” policy

This is a rule about data labels, not a ban on emotions. A recorded reply is an
observation; “they like Alex” is an interpretation. Do not present the latter as
ground truth. A person may feel, decline or change their mind without breaking
our model's rules.

The Romantic Turing Test evaluates whether a fictional bio sounds concrete and
stays faithful to its supplied case. It is not permission to impersonate someone
or conceal an automated account. Photos remain fictional teaching assets;
detection confidence and eye-contact duration are not measures of consent.

## Model claims

A toy Elo calculation is not recovered Hinge source code. A made-up response
probability is not a research finding. A Null Island percentile is not a
real-app ranking or an estimate of human worth.

State the data origin, assumptions, score and denominator alongside a result.
Count benchmark ties against the candidate. Preserve failed evaluations and
clearly label revisions made after observing them. We grade reproducibility and
reasoning, never romantic outcomes or a required rank.

An explicit refusal is a terminal state in response exercises. Silence is not
consent. A utility function does not create an obligation for another person.

## Assessment and handover

The report, exam and project carry **20%, 30% and 50%**. The report is due
**19 March, 5 pm**; the exam runs **23 April, 11 am–12.30 pm**; the project is due
**28 May, 5 pm**. All times are Canberra local time in 2027. The exam takes place
in Systems Lab 2.

In this fictional course, hand report/project bundles to Casimir on removable media
or arrange an accessible digital handover at consultation. This public website
has no login, upload, messaging or exam service. A project may use a public static
URL or a self-contained local build plus a recorded demo on the same marking
terms. This does not alter the actual website assignment's GitHub Pages requirement.

## Extensions, absence and review

For this invented course, request an extension or deferred exam from Casimir before
the due time where possible. If an unexpected circumstance prevents that, raise
it at the next consultation. An exam absence requires a deferred sitting, not a
late upload of answers.

Report/project work up to seven calendar days late without an arranged extension
receives feedback with a cap of 50% of available marks; beyond that an extension
is needed for a mark. These are fictional SLOP1276 rules, not ANU policy.
Attendance at practice labs has no separate mark.

Report and exam feedback returns within seven days; project feedback within
fourteen. Request review within seven calendar days of receiving feedback,
identifying the published criterion at issue. See the [teaching team](/people/)
for the fictional consultation arrangements.

## Sources, code and AI assistance

On reports, labs and the project, AI and external libraries may assist with code
or drafting. Cite sources, identify tools, record what they changed and verify
every calculation. Keep an account of rejected suggestions as well as accepted
ones. You remain responsible for explaining your submission.

During the midterm, no AI, internet, messaging or executable code is permitted.
Only a non-programmable calculator and your own double-sided A4 note sheet are
allowed. Do not submit someone else's working as yours.

Never upload private personal data to an external tool. Generated observations
must be labelled synthetic, not presented as a study or a real dating encounter.

## Access and support

Lecture text, CSV data and worked examples remain available without an account.
Request accessible assessment formats, extra exam access arrangements or a
text-only/solo lab review from Casimir or Joost. No explanation of your relationship
status is needed.

This is a technical course, not counselling. For real online-dating concerns,
[eSafety's guidance](https://www.esafety.gov.au/key-topics/staying-safe/online-dating)
is an external resource, not a SlopU service. You may stop an exercise or choose
an equivalent fictional case without disclosing why.
