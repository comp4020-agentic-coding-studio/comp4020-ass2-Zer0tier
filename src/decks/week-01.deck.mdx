# How to Find a Partner

SLOP1276 · Week 01

From a first hello to a mutual yes.

---

## Start with an ordinary Tuesday

What would sharing your life actually involve?

Study. Friends. Work. Time alone. Someone else’s plans.

---

## Want / offer / ask

- What relationship do I want?
- What can I honestly offer?
- What do I need to ask?

Three columns. No perfect answers.

---

## Meet Alex and Sam

Alex wants a relationship. Alex works evenings.

Sam likes meeting people. Sam is still deciding.

What can they assume about each other? Very little.

---

## An intention is speakable

“I’d like to get to know someone with the possibility of a relationship.”

What would you need to learn from the other person?

---

## Your turn · five minutes

Choose Alex, yourself, or an invented character.

Write one want, one thing you can offer, and one question.

Then check the plan against an ordinary week.

---

## The semester’s path

Know yourself → make a connection → choose together.

Every workshop adds one piece to your dating field guide.

Every next step leaves room for either person to stop.

---

## What earns a mark?

Intentions map · 20%

First-date plan · 30%

Dating field guide · 50%

Your choices and reasoning. Your relationship status is private.
