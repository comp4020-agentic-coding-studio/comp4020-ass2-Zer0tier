# Algorithmic Romance

The syllabus drop.

SLOP4276 · Week 01

Your love life has a loss function.

---

## The opening provocation

Love is not a mystery.

It is an inefficient market problem solvable through data structures.

Our job: implement the claim, then find where its types stop making sense.

---

## Three different outputs

A recommendation is not a reply.

A reply is not a relationship.

“Reply observed” is data.

“They like Alex” is an inference.

Returning HTTP 200 is not evidence of affection.

---

## Twelve weeks, one system

**1–3 · Measure:** introduction, platforms, photos.

**4–7 · Model:** bios, matches, messages, latency.

**8–12 · Ship:** threats, offline plans, first dates, follow-up, maintenance.

Each tutorial hands an artefact to the next.

---

## Strictly: “No Feelings”

Do not invent emotion labels in a dataset.

People still get to have feelings.

They can decline, leave or change their minds.

A refusal is terminal, not a transient error.

---

## Romantic Turing Test

A: “Seeking synergistic engagement.”

B: “Board games, bad puns, and coffee on Friday before seven.”

Which describes our fictional Alex?

Check the case facts. Do not invent a better person.

---

## Acquisition ethics

Use the supplied synthetic data.

Check provenance, permission, purpose and minimum fields.

No app scraping, private messages or real-user experiments.

Public visibility is not a harvesting licence.

---

## What earns a mark?

Data report · **20%** · 19 March

Midterm exam · **30%** · 23 April

Fictional profile release · **50%** · 28 May

Engineering evidence, not romantic outcomes or a required rank.
