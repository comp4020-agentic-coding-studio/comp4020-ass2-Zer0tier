## Our inspectable toy model

E = 1 / (1 + 10^((Q − R) / 400))

R′ = R + K × (S − E)

R and Q: artificial ratings.

S: a simulated result, 0 or 1.

This is not Hinge's algorithm.

---

## One update, by hand

R = Q = 1200; K = 32; S = 1.

Equal ratings give E = 0.5.

R′ = 1200 + 32 × 0.5 = **1216**.

Check the arithmetic before trusting the plot.

---

## Try manipulating K

Reset both ratings to 1200.

With a simulated win:

K = 16 → 1208. K = 64 → 1232.

The rating moved faster.

What evidence says more people connected? None.
