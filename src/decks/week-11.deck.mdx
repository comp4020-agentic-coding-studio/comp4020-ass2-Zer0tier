## The 100-entry contract

**99 controls + one candidate = 100**. Ties count against the candidate.

Only **rank 1/100** receives this benchmark’s label. Rank is not graded.

Controls are deliberately fabricated vectors. They predict no app rank or romantic outcome.

Do not round scores before counting ties.

## Answer · preserve what happened

**A:** prespecified sensitivity. **B:** labelled exploration.

**C:** misrepresented history. **D:** unsupported rating.

[scikit-learn’s leakage guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage) explains why evaluation information must not steer model choices.

A public synthetic benchmark never becomes a hidden test set merely because you froze a file.
