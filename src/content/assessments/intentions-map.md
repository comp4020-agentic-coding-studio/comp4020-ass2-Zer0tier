## The brief

Alex wants a committed relationship, works three evenings a week and has Thursday afternoons free. They like pottery and quiet walks. Their friends suggest going out every night until they meet someone. Give Alex a plan they could actually live with.

You may use Alex or invent an equivalent case. Do not submit real names, dating screenshots or somebody else's private messages.

## What you submit

One PDF of 600–800 words, including your three-column intentions map, three boundary statements, a comparison of three meeting routes and a chosen weekly plan. End with 100 words explaining one change made after workshop feedback. Tables count toward the word guide.

Explain why the chosen route fits the intention and constraint. A list of appealing venues without that connection is incomplete. A strong submission also explains why it rejected one option.

## Preparation and feedback

[Week 1](/sessions/01-intentions/) supplies the map, [week 2](/sessions/02-boundaries/) supplies the boundaries, and [week 3](/sessions/03-meeting-places/) tests the settings. Written feedback is returned within seven days of the due date, before you build the assessed invitation.

## Submission

In this fictional course, hand a printed copy to the convenor by 5 pm Canberra time on the due date. An accessible digital format can be arranged in consultation. This public prototype does not collect submissions. [Course policies](/policies/) cover extensions and review.

Your relationship status and any real-world dating outcome have no effect on your mark.
