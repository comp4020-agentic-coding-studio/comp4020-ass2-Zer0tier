## Before you arrive

Read the course policies and the fictional Alex and Sam case above. Bring three sentences describing an ordinary week for your chosen character.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-01/) for the worked example.

## In the room · 90 minutes

Spend 15 minutes separating wants from assumptions, 25 minutes drafting the three-column map, and 20 minutes checking it against your character’s actual week. Use the final 30 minutes to exchange feedback and revise one unrealistic expectation.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Intentions map.** A reader can identify one intention, one practical constraint and one unanswered question.

Keep the map: next week you will turn its limits into sentences another person can understand.
