## Before you arrive

Bring your week 1 intentions map. Read [What are my boundaries?](https://www.loveisrespect.org/resources/what-are-my-boundaries/) and select one everyday boundary to discuss.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-02/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Intentions map](/sessions/01-intentions/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Spend 20 minutes sorting six statements into preferences, boundaries or demands. Use 30 minutes to rewrite the time, privacy and affection limits from your map. In the next 25 minutes, a reader plays a person who wants something different. Finish with 15 minutes revising the response.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Boundary statements.** Three statements describe a limit and your character’s own action; each allows the other person to disagree.

Carry these statements into the meeting-place map: a place to meet has to fit your limits as well as your interests.
