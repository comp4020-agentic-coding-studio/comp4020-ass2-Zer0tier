## Before you arrive

Bring the intentions map and boundary statements. Invent or identify three possible meeting settings; no visit or app account is required.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-03/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Intentions map](/sessions/01-intentions/), [Boundary statements](/sessions/02-boundaries/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Compare the three settings for 25 minutes, use 25 minutes to draft two feasible weekly plans, and spend 25 minutes explaining one exclusion to a peer. Use the final 15 minutes to prepare the intentions-map assessment.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Meeting-place map.** Your map compares three routes and justifies a choice using a specific intention and boundary.

Use your chosen setting as the audience for next week’s introduction card.
