## Before you arrive

Bring compatibility questions and the repair dialogue. Decide what each character wants before drafting their conversation.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-11/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Compatibility questions](/sessions/09-compatibility/), [Repair dialogue](/sessions/10-disagreement/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Spend 20 minutes mapping each person’s expectations, 30 minutes writing the conversation, and 25 minutes testing whether agreement is explicit. Use 15 minutes to write the ending where the answers differ.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Relationship conversation.** Both voices state their wishes; both the agreement and mismatch endings preserve a choice.

Add both endings to the final field guide. You will be assessed on the reasoning in each branch.
