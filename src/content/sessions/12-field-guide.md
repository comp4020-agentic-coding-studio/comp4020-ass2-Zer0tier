## Before you arrive

Bring the accumulated guide, your earlier assessment feedback and the week 11 two-ending conversation. Read the final assessment checklist.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-12/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Intentions map](/sessions/01-intentions/), [First-date plan](/sessions/07-first-date/), [Uncertainty decision tree](/sessions/08-uncertainty/), [Relationship conversation](/sessions/11-define-it/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Use 20 minutes to trace the main path, 30 minutes to test Jo and Riley’s changed circumstance, and 25 minutes for a peer or solo review of the guide’s weakest transition. Reserve 15 minutes for the final revision plan.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Dating field guide.** The guide has a connected path, a justified stopping branch and a revision based on feedback.

Submit the field guide after the workshop. Keep it as a set of questions you can adapt, rather than a script another person must follow.
