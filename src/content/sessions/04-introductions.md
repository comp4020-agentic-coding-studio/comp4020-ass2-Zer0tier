## Before you arrive

Bring the week 3 meeting-place map. Choose either a spoken introduction or a fictional app-style profile, and list two true details about the character.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-04/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Meeting-place map](/sessions/03-meeting-places/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Draft for 20 minutes. Spend 25 minutes asking readers to underline what they could reply to. Use 25 minutes to remove an exaggeration and a privacy risk. In the final 20 minutes, compare the revision to the original.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Introduction card.** The introduction contains a concrete conversational opening and matches the setting and character.

The detail a reader picked becomes the opening line of your week 5 conversation.
