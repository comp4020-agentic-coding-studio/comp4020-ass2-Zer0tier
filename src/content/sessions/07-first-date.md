## Before you arrive

Bring the week 3 meeting-place map and week 6 yes branch. Read the meeting-in-person section of eSafety’s online dating guidance.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-07/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Meeting-place map](/sessions/03-meeting-places/), [Invitation branches](/sessions/06-invitations/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Spend 25 minutes planning logistics, 25 minutes checking access and budget, and 25 minutes responding to a venue cancellation. Use the last 15 minutes to incorporate feedback before assessment submission.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**First-date plan.** The plan includes mutual agreement, access, cost, travel, duration and an alternative.

Your week 8 case begins with the follow-up after this date.
