## Before you arrive

Bring the first-date plan and invitation branches. Write the observed facts of the follow-up case in one sentence, with no guessed motive.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-08/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Invitation branches](/sessions/06-invitations/), [First-date plan](/sessions/07-first-date/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Sort facts from interpretations for 20 minutes, draft the tree for 30 minutes, and spend 25 minutes testing it against no reply, a cancellation and a refusal. Use 15 minutes to remove a branch that depends on mind-reading.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Uncertainty decision tree.** Every action uses an observable fact; explicit refusal leads to no further invitation.

A clear yes to another date still leaves compatibility unanswered. That is next week’s work.
