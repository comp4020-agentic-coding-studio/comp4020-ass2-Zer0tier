## Checkpoints and debrief

- **Observation:** before 1086 the Week 10 branch is pending; its latest completed record is agreed and the meeting ended. A withdrawal remains visible. A declined invitation terminates regardless of assumed utility.
- **Evidence:** each rating has a quote and reason. A quote-presence check cannot validate the rating; missing venue detail stays missing even when a notebook plan exists.
- **Evaluation:** report score, number strictly above, ties, rank and denominator. Do not round before ranking or compare new-weight candidates with old-weight controls. Empty resolved denominators are undefined, not zero.
- **Changed case:** controls contain no source bios, so do not invent re-ratings for their availability. A newly worded invitation needs a new version and review. An impressive rank cannot override a failed constraint.
- **Release:** the primary failed target stays visible; all sixteen inputs and output files have a retained checksum record. Browser evidence identifies what was actually inspected. A checksum establishes byte identity against that record, not authorship, accessibility or consent.
