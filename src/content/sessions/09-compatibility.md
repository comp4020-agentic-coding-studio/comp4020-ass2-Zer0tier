## Before you arrive

Bring your intentions map and uncertainty tree. Draft three questions that could change your character’s decision to keep dating.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-09/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Intentions map](/sessions/01-intentions/), [Uncertainty decision tree](/sessions/08-uncertainty/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Use 25 minutes to test the questions for hidden preferred answers, 25 minutes to write two different responses from Sam, and 25 minutes to explain what Alex can choose in each case. Spend 15 minutes revising the original intentions map.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Compatibility questions.** Three open questions connect to an intention; one response reveals a workable difference and one a mismatch.

Keep the workable difference. In week 10 you will practise discussing it without making a character accusation.
