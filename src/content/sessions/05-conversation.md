## Before you arrive

Bring the introduction card. Write one interested response and one brief response to its conversational opening.

Read [this week’s lecture](/lectures/week-05/) for the worked example.

Bring forward: [Introduction card](/sessions/04-introductions/).

## In the room · 90 minutes

Spend 20 minutes writing two possible starts, 30 minutes rehearsing or silently annotating an eight-turn conversation, and 25 minutes revising a follow-up that ignored the answer. Use 15 minutes to write a friendly exit.

You can role-play with a willing classmate or write both sides of the fictional exchange on your own. Feedback concerns the choices on the page; nobody needs to describe their dating life.

## What leaves the room

**Conversation transcript.** The transcript includes a relevant follow-up, reciprocal sharing and a clear way to end.

Keep the final exchange: in week 6 you will decide whether an invitation belongs there.
