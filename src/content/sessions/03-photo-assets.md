## Inputs

Bring the **Platform audit** and three fictional photo descriptions or placeholder drawings. Use no identifiable real people.

## 90-minute tutorial

1. **20 minutes — define fields.** Record asset ID, provenance, permission, intended subject, crop ambiguity and alt text. Keep detection confidence separate from identity certainty.
2. **20 minutes — calculate.** Use 200/100 for the lighting ratio and the lecture's invented 6/10, 2/10 and 3/10 identification failures. Label all figures synthetic.
3. **25 minutes — challenge the metric.** Construct a 0.98-confidence group-photo case that still fails subject identification. Flag one ambiguous crop among three assets; do not invent the cropped person's relationship.
4. **25 minutes — select and freeze.** Pick the clearest supported asset, preserve two rejected alternatives and write an acceptance check for missing provenance.

## Deliverable: Photo asset manifest

Save three records, the selected asset ID, calculations and a short reason for each rejection. A text-only placeholder with informative alt text is sufficient; artistic skill is not assessed.

Week 4 must hold this photo choice constant while comparing bios. That dependency prevents a photo change from masquerading as a bio effect.
