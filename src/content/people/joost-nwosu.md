Dr Joost Nwosu is a Postdoctoral Fellow in Slop University's
[School of Continuous Improvement](https://slop.university/schools/continuous-improvement/).
His research follows what happens after a performance target is reached,
including how benchmarks become lasting expectations. See his
[university profile](https://slop.university/people/joost-nwosu/) for his
research and university role.

## What to bring to Joost

Bring the smallest input that reproduces the error, the result you expected and
the result you got. Joost runs the tutorials, checks experiment protocols and helps
trace disagreements between exact calculations and simulations.

You may implement in any language. A clear dependency list and readable tests
matter more than a fashionable framework. Ask for a text-based or solo review
format whenever needed; no real-user experiment is part of the course.

Assessment marks, extensions and exam arrangements go to
[Casimir Beng](/people/casimir-beng/). Joost's university role comes from SlopU's
staff directory; his tutoring duties and consultation hours here belong to this
fictional course. This is not a live support service or dating consultation.
