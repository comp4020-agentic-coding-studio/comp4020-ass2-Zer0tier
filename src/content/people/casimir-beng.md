Associate Professor Casimir Beng leads the Adaptive Metrics Lab in Slop
University's [School of Continuous Improvement](https://slop.university/schools/continuous-improvement/).
His research examines how measurement changes the systems being measured,
including cases where those changes do not amount to improvement.
See his [university profile](https://slop.university/people/casimir-beng/) for
his research and university role.

## What to bring to Casimir

Bring your frozen objective, an identification problem or a proposed
assessment change. Casimir leads this course's lectures and final technical
defence. Be ready to explain what would make your conclusion false.

For a mark review, bring the marked work and the criterion you want reconsidered.
For an extension or deferred exam, propose a workable date. Personal relationship
details are not required to explain a course-design or access issue.

His university role comes from SlopU's staff directory. The teaching duties,
room and consultation hours here describe this fictional course; the prototype
has no live contact or booking service.
